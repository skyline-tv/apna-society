Duplicates and inconsistencies

  • Layout Live PDFs are unstyled Helvetica text. Local Flutter PDFs are navy/blue cards. In-app screens are a third Material layout.
  • Page size Backend uses US Letter (612×792). Flutter local receipts use A4.
  • Brand colour App tokens are deep blue #1B4F8A. Local receipts use #1E4DB7. Live PDFs have no colour. Proposed design now uses brand blue.
  • Collections Reuse the maintenance PDF builder and overload billNumber with the collection title.
  • Safety Receipts are issued only for SUCCESS payments. FAILED / PENDING never get a receipt. Receipt numbers are unique per society.
  • Interest Maintenance receipts can show allocated interest. Collection receipts do not. Billing math is unchanged in this review.

Not payment receipts

bill_pdf.dart (bills), report_pdf.dart, and collection admin report PDFs are reports or invoices, not payment receipts.
